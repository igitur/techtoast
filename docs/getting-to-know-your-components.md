# Getting to know your components:

The information below does not need to be memorised to build circuits, and as you build more circuits you will naturally learn the important parts.

1. **ESP32 Development Board:** The ESP32 is a powerful microcontroller with built-in **Wi-Fi** and **Bluetooth** capabilities. It features a **dual-core processor**, ample GPIO pins, and support for protocols like SPI, I2C, and UART. Ideal for IoT projects, automation systems, and more, the ESP32 offers high performance and low power consumption.
2. **Double-Width Breadboard:** This full-size breadboard allows you to prototype circuits with ease. Featuring **830 tie points**, it provides plenty of space for connecting components and testing your ideas without soldering.
3. **USB Programming Cable:** A durable USB cable designed for uploading code and powering your ESP32 development board. It ensures reliable data transmission and is compatible with most USB ports.
4. **IR Obstacle Avoidance Module:** Detect obstacles easily with this IR sensor module. It uses infrared light to detect objects and is ideal for robotic navigation and line-following applications.
5. **Soil Humidity Sensor:** Monitor soil moisture levels with this sensor, perfect for smart gardening projects. It detects water content in the soil and outputs an analog or digital signal.
6. **Sound Sensor:** This module detects sound intensity in the environment. It's great for voice-activated projects, noise monitoring, and audio-based applications.
7. **Potentiometer:** A 10KΩ variable resistor used to adjust voltage or signal levels. Commonly used for dimming LEDs, controlling volume, or ﬁne-tuning sensors.
8. **Rain/Water Level Sensor:** Detect rain or water levels with this versatile sensor. Its conductive tracks measure water presence, making it perfect for weather monitoring or liquid level detection.
9. **7-Segment Display:** This numeric display module is ideal for creating digital counters, clocks, or measurement readouts. Easy to control with the ESP32 for clear numeric output.
10. **DHT11 Temperature and Humidity Sensor:** A reliable sensor for measuring temperature and humidity. Outputs digital signals, making it simple to use for weather stations or climate monitoring.
11. **Passive Buzzer:** This buzzer generates sound when driven with varying frequencies, perfect for melodies or tone alerts in your projects.
12. **Active Buzzer:** A self-contained buzzer that emits a continuous tone when powered, ideal for alarms or notiﬁcations.
13. **Push Buttons (6x):** These momentary push buttons are ideal for user input. Use them in various applications like triggering actions or navigating menus.
14. **Thermistor:** A temperature-sensitive resistor that changes resistance with temperature variations. Perfect for temperature monitoring and control systems.
15. **220Ω Resistors:** These resistors are commonly used for limiting current to LEDs, ensuring they operate safely.
16. **1kΩ Resistors:** These resistors are useful for pull-up/pull-down circuits and other general-purpose applications.
17. **10kΩ Resistors:** These resistors are useful for pull-up/pull-down circuits and other general-purpose applications.
18. **Male-to-Male Jumper Wires (10x):** Essential for connecting components on the breadboard or between the ESP32 and other modules.
19. **Male-to-Female Jumper Wires (10x):** Perfect for connecting modules or sensors to the ESP32 development board.
20. **Photoresistors (LDR) (2x):** Light-dependent resistors that change resistance based on light intensity. Ideal for light-sensing projects like night lights or solar trackers.
21. **Tilt Switch:** Detect angular changes with this switch, often used in orientation-based triggers or safety cutoffs.
22. **RGB LED:** A single LED capable of displaying a wide range of colours by combining red, green, and blue light. Create dynamic lighting effects or status indicators.
23. **Red LEDs:** Standard red LEDs for indicators or decorative lighting.
24. **Green LEDs:** Bright green LEDs perfect for status lights or power indicators.
25. **Yellow LEDs:** Versatile yellow LEDs ideal for traffic light simulations or warning indicators.
26. **PDF Instruction Manual QR Code Card:** A convenient QR code card that links to the PDF manual for detailed setup instructions and project ideas.
27. **Resistor Colour Card:** This handy reference card helps you quickly identify resistor values based on their colour bands.
